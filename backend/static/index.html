Questions

Is AudionLabs free?

Yes. Every tool on the site is free to use, with no account and no watermark on your exports.

Do my files get uploaded?

The Slowed + Reverb studio runs entirely in your browser, so those files never leave your device. Stem splitting and transcription need large AI models, so those files are sent to our server, processed, and kept only temporarily so you can download your results.

Why does the pitch drop when I slow a track down?

The studio changes speed the way a turntable or tape deck does. Slowing the playback lowers the pitch with it, and that is the sound slowed + reverb edits are known for. Speeding up raises the pitch in the same way, which is how nightcore edits are made.

Can I slow down only the vocals?

Yes. Split the song in the Stem Splitter, solo the vocals in the live mixer, then choose Open mix in Slowed + Reverb. Your vocal-only mix loads into the studio, ready to edit.

Which file types work?

The live studio opens anything your browser can play, including MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, and FLAC. The Stem Splitter takes MP3 and WAV. Transcribe takes MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, and FLAC.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every tool works in a mobile browser. Very long tracks use a lot of memory in the live mixer, so a computer is the better choice for anything over ten minutes.
