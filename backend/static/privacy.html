Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

The short version

AudionLabs does not require an account. We do not sell your files or use them to train AI models. Some tools run entirely in your browser, and some send your file to our server to be processed.

Files processed in your browser

The Slowed + Reverb studio and the live stem mixer run on your device. When you open a file in the studio, it is read by your browser and is never uploaded to us. A mix you send from the stem mixer to the studio is held briefly in your browser's local storage and removed as soon as the studio opens it.

Files processed on our server

Stem splitting and transcription need large AI models, so those files are uploaded to our server. We use them only to produce your results. Uploaded files and results are temporary. They are not attached to any account or identity, and they are not shared with anyone.

Transcription summaries are generated by sending the transcript text to Anthropic's Claude API. The YouTube tools fetch the link you provide from YouTube on your behalf.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and network providers record standard request information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. We use this to keep the service running and to prevent abuse.

Advertising and cookies

AudionLabs may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. You can turn off personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and you can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Services we rely on

  • Railway hosts the application.
  • Cloudflare provides the network, security, and caching in front of it.
  • Anthropic generates transcription summaries.
  • Google may serve advertising.

Children

AudionLabs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
